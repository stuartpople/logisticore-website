Multimodal Freight Forwarding Software

Air, sea, road and courier should share one operational truth

Mode-specific tools create mode-specific headaches

Forwarders rarely sell only one mode. Customers mix air and ocean, add road delivery, or switch to courier for urgent parcels. If each mode lives in a different system, your team rebuilds the same parties, charges and references again and again.

Multimodal freight forwarding software keeps the commercial and operational story in one place while still supporting mode-specific documents and fields.

What “multimodal” should mean in practice

  • Air: MAWB/HAWB, airline rates, consolidation, flight context
  • Sea: FCL/LCL, BL workflows, container and voyage awareness
  • Road: CMR, POD, domestic and cross-border movements
  • Courier: express labels, carrier tracking, last-mile updates
  • Shared layer: parties, charges, documents, tasks, CRM and billing

Questions to ask in every multimodal demo

  1. Can one job file support a mode change mid-shipment without starting over?
  2. Do consolidations preserve house and master relationships cleanly?
  3. Are documents mode-aware but generated from the same master data?
  4. Does finance see one cost/revenue picture across modes?
  5. Can customers track the whole journey in one portal experience?

Where LogistiCore fits

LogistiCore SaaS is built as multimodal freight software with shared job management, documents, warehouse, CRM, billing and AI assist. UK customs via HMRC CDS is available inside the same platform for teams that file UK declarations — a regional capability on top of international operations.
