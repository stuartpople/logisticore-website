How to Migrate from a Legacy Freight TMS

A practical cutover plan for UK forwarders

Updated 28 July 2026

Replacing a legacy TMS feels risky because freight never stops. The teams that succeed treat migration as an operations project, not an IT weekend.

Phase 1 — Discovery

  • List modes you actually run (air export, sea import, road, courier, warehouse)
  • Inventory integrations: accounting, carriers, email, customs
  • Score pain: re-keying, reporting gaps, CDS friction, DG handling

Phase 2 — Pilot

Configure LogistiCore SaaS (or your shortlisted platform) for a thin slice of live work. Use real customers and real documents. Measure time-to-book and error rates against the legacy system.

Phase 3 — Data

  1. Cleanse customer and supplier masters
  2. Map charge codes and VAT treatments
  3. Import open jobs; archive completed history on a schedule

Phase 4 — Training and go-live

Role-based sessions for ops, customs, finance and sales. Keep hypercare coverage for the first two weeks. Pair with our buyer’s shortlist guide if you are still choosing a platform.
