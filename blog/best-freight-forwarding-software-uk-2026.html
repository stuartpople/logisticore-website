Best Freight Forwarding Software UK 2026

How to choose a logistics TMS that covers jobs, customs, and billing

Updated 28 July 2026 · 8 min read

UK freight forwarders searching for the best freight forwarding software in 2026 face a crowded market: legacy on-prem TMS platforms, generic ERPs with logistics bolt-ons, and modern cloud SaaS built for multimodal operations. This guide explains how to shortlist properly — without buying on brochure features alone.

What “best” means for a UK forwarder

“Best” is not a single product ranking. It is the platform that matches your modes, compliance burden, and team size with the lowest total cost of ownership. Score vendors against your real workflows: air consolidations, sea FCL/LCL, road collections, courier labels, warehouse handovers, and UK customs declarations.

Must-have modules in 2026

  • Multimodal job management — air (AWB), sea (BL/VGM), road, and courier in one job lifecycle
  • HMRC CDS customs — native or tightly integrated declarations, validation, and audit trail
  • Dangerous goods (DG) — checks that match IATA/IMDG workflows, not afterthought fields
  • Commercials — quotes, costs, invoices, and nominal posting without double-keying
  • Documents & visibility — attachments, milestones, and customer-facing status
  • Reporting — margin, volume, and exception dashboards your managers will actually use

CDS and UK compliance: a hard filter

If your brokers file UK import/export declarations, disconnected customs tools create re-keying and error risk. Prefer freight forwarding software where CDS data flows from the job — parties, goods lines, values, and transport details — with validation before submission. See our CDS customs declarations checklist for a go-live readiness list.

How to run a fair software shortlist

  1. Map processes — pick 5–10 representative shipments (air export, sea import, road collection, DG, bonded warehouse).
  2. Script demos — insist vendors execute your scenarios, not a canned happy path.
  3. Score TCO — licences, implementation, training, integrations, and support — not list price alone.
  4. Check migration — customer masters, open jobs, rate cards, and historical docs.
  5. Pilot — a limited live pilot beats a 40-slide RFP response.

Cloud SaaS vs legacy TMS

Legacy systems often win on “we’ve always used it.” Modern logistics SaaS usually wins on speed of change: CDS updates, AI assist for documents, mobile-friendly ops, and continuous releases without weekend upgrades. FAQs

What is the best freight forwarding software for UK companies in 2026?

Shortlist platforms that cover your modes plus CDS, then score live demos on your own shipments. The winner is the system your ops team can run day one without spreadsheets.

Does freight forwarding software need HMRC CDS support?

Yes if you file UK declarations. Integrated CDS reduces re-keying and audit risk versus a separate customs desk tool.

How long does migration take?

Most SME forwarders complete a phased migration in weeks to a few months, depending on data quality and integrations.
