HMRC CDS Software for Freight Forwarders

Why UK customs should live inside your logistics platform

Updated 28 July 2026

UK freight forwarders searching for HMRC CDS software usually need more than a standalone declaration form. You need accurate parties, goods lines, values and transport details — the same data your ops team already holds in the job.

What “good” CDS software looks like for forwarders

  • Job-linked data — shipper, consignee, goods and invoices pull from the shipment, not a second spreadsheet
  • Validation before submit — catch missing tariff, origin or valuation issues early
  • Audit trail — who changed what, and which message versions went to HMRC
  • Broker and forwarder roles — clear handoffs when customs is filed in-house or via a partner

Separate customs desk vs TMS-native CDS

A dedicated customs tool can work for pure brokers. For multimodal forwarders, disconnected CDS systems create double entry and reconciliation work. The stronger pattern in 2026 is a logistics SaaS platform where CDS sits next to air, sea, road and documents.

Checklist before you buy

  1. Map five real declarations from last month into the demo
  2. Ask how invoice lines and commercial values enter the declaration
  3. Confirm error handling and amendment workflows
  4. Review our CDS customs checklist
