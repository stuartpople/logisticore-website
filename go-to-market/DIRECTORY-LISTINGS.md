## Full description

LogistiCore SaaS is an all-in-one freight forwarding platform built for logistics teams worldwide.

Forwarders and 3PLs use LogistiCore to run air, sea, road, rail and courier shipments from enquiry through invoicing. The platform includes job management, transport document generation, rates and quotations, consolidations, warehouse activity, CRM, double-entry accounting, carrier tracking, compliance tools, AI assistance and a white-label customer portal.

LogistiCore is UK-headquartered. For UK customs operations, it includes real-time HMRC CDS declarations (import, export and corrections), tariff lookup and EORI validation — integrated into the operational workflow rather than treated as a disconnected bolt-on.

Typical buyers: independent freight forwarders, customs brokers, air cargo agents, NVOCCs, consolidators and growing 3PLs that want modern cloud software without enterprise complexity theatre.
