Short boilerplate

LogistiCore is cloud freight software for international forwarders, customs brokers and 3PLs. Teams manage multimodal jobs, documents, warehouse, billing, tracking and AI assist in one platform. LogistiCore is UK-headquartered and includes direct HMRC CDS customs capability for UK declarations.

Longer boilerplate

LogistiCore Technologies builds LogistiCore SaaS, an all-in-one freight forwarding platform with 200+ features covering air, sea, road, rail and courier operations. The platform connects job management, transport documents, rates, consolidations, warehouse activity, accounting, CRM, compliance and a white-label customer portal. For UK customs work, LogistiCore integrates with HMRC CDS for real-time declarations. Complementary IT support and website services are available for logistics businesses that want one technology partner.
