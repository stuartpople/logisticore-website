# LogistiCore business cards — print brief

## Specs to order
- Size: UK standard 85 × 55 mm (or MOO Standard)
- Stock: 380–400gsm soft-touch / velvet laminate (matte)
- Finish: Soft Touch both sides
- Accent (optional): cyan or silver foil on logo mark + thin rule only (keep foil under ~20% coverage)
- Sides: double-sided
- Quantity: 250 or 500

## Colour
- Navy: #0A1628
- Cyan accent: #00BCD4
- Text: #FFFFFF
- Secondary text: light cyan / soft grey

## Preferred concept
1. Navy soft-touch (primary) — front wordmark + cube; back contact details
2. White quiet-luxury alt — if you want a lighter card for daytime meetings

## Artwork files
Mockups in this folder are for direction. For print, export CMYK PDF with 3mm bleed from a designer or MOO’s upload tool. Logo source: `../img/logisticore-technologies-logo.png`

## Recommended printers (UK)
1. MOO Super Soft Touch + Silver Foil — fastest premium path
2. After Hours Creative — 400gsm velvet + hot foil (more “boardroom”)
3. Instantprint specialty soft-touch — strong value if ordering 500+
