Can freight and warehouse live in one system?

Yes. LogistiCore SaaS is built so a forwarder or 3PL can run the shipment and the warehouse task from the same operational record. Air, sea, road and courier jobs, documents, billing and CRM sit alongside warehouse activity. Customs is included in that same record, with UK HMRC CDS live today and the customs platform being extended worldwide. LogistiCore Technologies Ltd is UK-headquartered and sells to logistics teams worldwide.

  • Who it is for: forwarders and 3PLs that move and store freight.
  • Who it is not for: a warehouse-only WMS with no freight job, or a customs tool with no operations.
  • Guide prices, 3 users included: from £349, £549 or £799 per month.
